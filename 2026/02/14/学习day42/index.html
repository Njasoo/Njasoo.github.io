洛谷 P2261 [CQOI2007] 余数求和

题意: 计算

$$ G(n, k) = \sum_{i = 1} ^ {n} {k \space mod \space i} $$

思路:

$$ a \bmod b = a - \lfloor a/b \rfloor \cdot b \\ \sum_{i=1}^{n} k \bmod i = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (k - \lfloor k/i \rfloor) \cdot i = n \cdot k - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \lfloor k/i \rfloor \cdot i $$
